      Rules of engagement

      • Everyone starts with 3 hearts, 0 AP and a range of 2, somewhere random on the map.
      • Your hearts are secret. Nobody else sees them, and only you and your target see a hit land. A kill is public.
      • Everyone gets 1 AP per day. AP never expires. Spend it whenever you like.
      • Move one tile in any of 8 directions: 1 AP. You can't drive onto another tank.
      • Attack a tank in range: 1 AP. It loses a heart.
      • Give 1 AP to a tank in range: costs you 1 AP.
      • Extend your range by 1: 2 AP. No cap. Range is a square, so diagonals count.
      • Lose all 3 hearts and you're out. No jury, no revivals. Last tank standing wins.
      • Comms go to everyone, or privately to one player.
      • Online, AP drops once a day at the host's chosen time. Locally, each full round is a day.
